# Comparison

## Similar tools

- Expensify and Dext turn receipts into expense reports and accounting entries. They are polished and cloud-first.
- [ReceiptProcessor](https://github.com/maxhightower/ReceiptProcessor) shows the open-source OCR-to-Excel shape.
- Spreadsheet import presets are the difference between a demo and a finance workflow.

## What snap2sheet does differently

- A local batch scanner with deterministic parsing and optional local LLM refinement.
- CSV and XLSX output with formula-injection protection.
- Duplicate detection that keeps the duplicate visible instead of silently dropping it.
- QuickBooks and Xero CSV presets.
- Multi-page PDFs are OCRed together instead of only the first page.
- No account, upload, or subscription.

## Tradeoff

Expensify and Dext cover approvals, cards, and accounting sync. snap2sheet covers the extraction and review step, and leaves the accounting system to the user.
